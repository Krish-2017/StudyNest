Operating Systems

Unit IV — Deadlocks & Memory Management

1. Deadlock: Introduction

A deadlock is a situation in which a set of processes are blocked because each process is holding a resource and waiting for another resource acquired by another process.

Necessary conditions

Mutual exclusion · Hold and wait · No preemption · Circular wait

Exam tip

Always draw the resource-allocation graph before applying the Banker's Algorithm.

About StudyNest

A calmer place to study.

StudyNest is a student-first resource hub designed for MDU Rohtak B.Tech learners.

Are these notes official MDU course materials?

No. Resources are community-contributed study aids. Use them alongside the official syllabus and materials provided by your faculty.

How do I upload a resource?

Choose Upload Notes, add the file and its course details, then submit it for review. Approved resources are listed publicly.

Can I use the AI assistant for exam answers?

Use it to learn and revise, but verify important answers, formulas, and syllabus coverage with your prescribed materials and faculty guidance.

What needs configuring for a live deployment?

Connect authentication, file storage, a database, and a moderation workflow. For AI, call OpenAI or Gemini from a server-side endpoint using environment variables—never browser-side API keys.
